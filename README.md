## Enquiry Form
The website includes an enquiry form configured with FormSubmit to forward submissions to the company email. On first use, FormSubmit may send a one-time activation/confirmation email to the destination address. After activation, future submissions are forwarded automatically.

For production, consider replacing FormSubmit with a backend/serverless email provider for stronger control, spam protection and delivery monitoring.
